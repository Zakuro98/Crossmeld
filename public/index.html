How to Play

Crossmeld is a 2–4 player tile game about building melds, completing them, and racing to go out. The goal is to score points across rounds until you reach the target score, and finish the match with the highest total.

On your turn

1

Draw two tiles from the stock, or take the discard pile if its top tile can be used immediately.

2

Optionally, create new melds or add tiles to your existing melds. Only you can modify your own melds, although everyone can see them.

3

End your turn by discarding one tile. If you legally go out by melding your last tiles, no discard is needed.

Tile types

There are four kinds of tiles: natural tiles, blank tiles, white tiles, and black tiles.

Natural tileA colored numbered tile.
Blank tileWild number. It keeps its color, but can stand for any number in that color.
White tileWild color. It keeps its number, but can stand for any color of that number.
Black tileBlocks discard-pile pickup while on top. Three or more black tiles can only be melded as a final going-out play.

Completions

A meld is completed when it covers all six identities for its axis.

  • A number meld is completed when it contains all 6 colors.
  • A color meld is completed when it contains all 6 numbers.
  • A completion is natural if it uses no wilds, and mixed if it uses at least one wild.
Natural completionAll six numbers in Yellow, with no wilds.
Mixed completionA completed 3s meld that uses wilds to cover missing identities.

Going out

  • To go out, you must first have the required number of completed ordinary melds.
  • In a 2-player game, you need 2 completed ordinary melds.
  • In a 3- or 4-player game, you need 1 completed ordinary meld.
  • You can go out either by discarding your final tile or by melding your final tiles and ending with an empty hand.
  • If you made no melds on earlier turns that round and then go out on one final turn, that is a concealed going out.

Scoring

Natural tile in a meld+5
Special tile in a meld (blank, white, or black)+10
Natural completion+100
Mixed completion+60
Going out+50
Concealed going out+100
Tiles left in your hand at round endSubtract their tile values

The match does not end mid-round. After each round, if at least one player has reached the target score, the game ends and the highest total wins.

Building melds

  • A meld must have at least 3 tiles.
  • A meld is either all the same number or all the same color.
  • Duplicates are allowed. For example, two Red 4s can be in a 4s meld.
  • An ordinary meld must contain at least 2 natural tiles.
  • An ordinary meld can contain at most 2 wild tiles.
Number meldAll tiles share the same number.
Color meldAll tiles share the same color.

Taking the discard pile

  • Taking the discard pile means taking every tile in it. You must use its top tile immediately as part of a meld.
  • If the pile is not frozen, you may add the top tile to one of your existing melds.
  • To use the top tile to start a new meld, you must already have two matching natural tiles in your hand.
  • If a black tile is on top, you cannot take the pile. Once another tile covers it, it no longer blocks pickup.
  • If a blank tile or white tile is on top, you also cannot take the pile.
  • A pile is frozen if it contains a blank tile or white tile anywhere, even when that wild tile is buried. It stays frozen until somebody takes it.
  • A frozen pile can only be taken when a natural tile is on top, by creating a new natural meld with two matching natural tiles already in your hand. You cannot use an existing meld.
Ordinary discard pileA natural tile on top, and no wild tiles in the pile. It can be taken using an existing meld or a legal new meld.
Blocked discard pileA black tile on top prevents pickup until it is covered.
Frozen discard pileA natural tile is on top, but a wild tile is buried inside. This pile can only be taken by starting a new natural meld with two matching natural tiles from your hand.
